Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

01Who We Are

This privacy policy covers the website of Toby Renfors trading as Tork Digital (ABN: 96 344 238 441), based in South Australia. It explains what information this site collects, why, and what happens to it.

02What We Collect

The only personal information this website collects is what you choose to submit through the enquiry form: your name, your email address, the enquiry category you select, and your message.

That is the complete list. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and uses no advertising or tracking technology of any kind. There is nothing to accept or opt out of, because nothing is collected in the background.

03How Enquiries Are Handled

Form submissions are processed by Netlify, the platform that hosts this website, and delivered to us by email. Submissions are stored in our Netlify dashboard and in our email inbox. Netlify applies automated spam filtering to submissions as part of this service.

We use your enquiry details for one purpose: to respond to you about your enquiry. Your details are never sold, shared for marketing, or added to any mailing list.

04Retention & Deletion

Enquiry submissions are kept only as long as they are useful for responding to you and for records of any resulting engagement, and are periodically cleared from our systems.

If you would like the details of an enquiry deleted at any time, send a request through the enquiry form and we will remove them.

05External Links

This site links to websites we have built for clients. Those sites are operated by their respective owners and have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

06The Legal Bit

We handle personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. This policy is governed by the laws of South Australia, Australia.
